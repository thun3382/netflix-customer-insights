# Netflix Customer Insights – Qualitative Research Study

## Overview
This research explores how long-term users form emotional and functional relationships with subscription platforms. Conducted an in-depth qualitative interview to explore the emotional and behavioral dynamics of customer-brand relationships, focusing on a long-term Netflix user. Mapped the customer's journey from early adoption to potential churn, identifying key drivers such as ad-free experience, original content, trust evolution, and emotional attachment. Developed strategic implications around price sensitivity, platform loyalty, and brand expansion opportunities. Applied coding methodology to synthesize themes and inform future consumer research. This individual project was part of MKT512: Customer Insights and Analysis course at USC Marshall School of Business.

## Key Takeaways
- Ad-free streaming and early innovation drive loyalty
- Emotional bonds form from shared experiences
- Price increases may threaten long-term commitment

## Tools Used
- Qualitative Interviewing
- Thematic Coding
- Customer Journey Mapping
